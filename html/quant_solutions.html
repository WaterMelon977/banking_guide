Wrong-number series questions: identify the one term that breaks the pattern and select it from the options.

Q46. 20, 24, 51, 115, 240, 456, 799

  1. 24
  2. 20
  3. 51
  4. 115
  5. 240
▶ Solution

Observe the pattern:

$$+2^3,\quad +3^3,\quad +4^3,\quad +5^3,\quad +6^3,\quad +7^3$$

Verification:

$$24+27=51$$ $$51+64=115$$ $$115+125=240$$ $$240+216=456$$ $$456+343=799$$

Hence, the first term should be:

$$24-8=16$$

The given first term is 20, which is incorrect.

✓ Correct Option: (b) 20

Q47. 24, 6, 12, 10, 28, 62, 194

  1. 62
  2. 10
  3. 12
  4. 24
  5. 6
▶ Solution

$$\times2-1,\quad \times3+1$$

$$6\times2-1=11$$ $$11\times3+1=34$$ $$34\times2-1=67$$ $$67\times3+1=202$$ $$202\times2-1=403$$ $$403\times3+1=1210$$

Thus, the fourth term should be 16, not 10.

✓ Correct Option: (b) 10

Question 68 (Set 4)

I. $x^2 - 18x + 65 = 0$

II. $y^2 - 29y + 208 = 0$

  1. $x > y$
  2. $x \ge y$
  3. $x < y$
  4. $x \le y$
  5. $x = y$ or relation cannot be established
▶ Solution

Equation I:

$$x^2 - 18x + 65 = 0$$ $$(x - 5)(x - 13) = 0$$ $$x = 5, 13$$

Equation II:

$$y^2 - 29y + 208 = 0$$ $$(y - 13)(y - 16) = 0$$ $$y = 13, 16$$

Comparison:

When $x = 5$: $5 < 13$ and $5 < 16$

When $x = 13$: $13 = 13$ and $13 < 16$

Thus, $x \le y$.

✓ Correct Option: (d) $x \le y$

Question 69 (Set 3)

What will be the ratio of savings of V and D?

Statement I: Income of V is 4% less than S. Expenditure ratio of V and S is 7 : 8. D spends $\frac{3}{5}$ of his income.

Statement II: S saves Rs. 14000 and V saves Rs. 14800. Income of D is Rs. 2000 more than S.

  1. The data in statement I alone is sufficient to answer the question, while the data in statement II alone is not sufficient to answer the question.
  2. The data in statement II alone is sufficient to answer the question, while the data in statement I alone is not sufficient to answer the question.
  3. The data either in statement I alone or in statement II alone is sufficient to answer the question.
  4. The data given in both statements I and II together are not sufficient to answer the question.
  5. The data in both statements I and II together are necessary to answer the question.
▶ Solution

From Statement I:

Let income of S be $100x$. Then income of V $= 96x$.

Ratio of expenditure of V and S $= 7 : 8$.

D spends $\frac{3}{5}$ of his income, so D saves $\frac{2}{5}$ of his income.

However, we do not have absolute values or D's income relative to V or S. Statement I alone is not sufficient.

From Statement II:

Savings of S $= 14000$, Savings of V $= 14800$.

Income of D $= \text{Income of S} + 2000$.

Statement II alone does not give expenditures or ratios. Statement II alone is not sufficient.

Combining Statements I and II:

From I & II:

$$\text{Income of V} - \text{Expenditure of V} = 14800$$ $$96x - 7y = 14800$$ $$\text{Income of S} - \text{Expenditure of S} = 14000$$ $$100x - 8y = 14000$$

Solving these two linear equations gives unique values for $x$ and $y$.

Once $x$ is known, Income of S $= 100x$ is known.

Then Income of D $= 100x + 2000$ is known.

Savings of D $= \frac{2}{5} \times \text{Income of D}$ is known.

Savings of V is given as $14800$.

Thus, the ratio of savings of V and D can be uniquely determined.

Both statements together are necessary.

✓ Correct Option: (e) The data in both statements I and II together are necessary to answer the question.

Question 70 (Set 4)

What is the age of R after two years?

Statement I: Average age of A and N is 24 years. Ratio of age of R and A is 2 : 3.

Statement II: N is 4 years older than S. Ratio of age of S and R is 1 : 2.

  1. The data in statement I alone is sufficient...
  2. The data in statement II alone is sufficient...
  3. The data either in statement I alone or in statement II alone is sufficient...
  4. The data given in both statements I and II together are not sufficient...
  5. The data in both statements I and II together are necessary...
▶ Solution

From Statement I:

$$A + N = 48$$ $$\frac{R}{A} = \frac{2}{3} \Rightarrow A = 1.5R$$

Not sufficient alone (N is unknown).

From Statement II:

$$N = S + 4$$ $$\frac{S}{R} = \frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow R = 2S$$

Not sufficient alone.

Combining Statements I and II:

We have:

$$A = 1.5R = 1.5(2S) = 3S$$ $$N = S + 4$$

Substitute into $A + N = 48$:

$$3S + S + 4 = 48$$ $$4S = 44 \Rightarrow S = 11$$ $$R = 2S = 22$$

Age of R after two years $= 22 + 2 = 24$ years.

Both statements together are necessary.

✓ Correct Option: (e) The data in both statements I and II together are necessary to answer the question.

Q57 (Set 6)

If LCM of 16, X and 40 is 160, then find the value of X.

Statement I: X is an even number.

Statement II: X is a multiple of 5.

  1. The data in statement I alone is sufficient...
  2. The data in statement II alone is sufficient...
  3. The data either in statement I alone or in statement II alone is sufficient...
  4. The data given in both statements I and II together are not sufficient...
  5. The data in both statements I and II together are necessary...
▶ Solution

Given:

$$\text{LCM}(16, X, 40) = 160$$

Prime factorizations:

$$16 = 2^4$$ $$40 = 2^3 \times 5$$ $$160 = 2^5 \times 5$$

For the LCM to contain $2^5$, $X$ must contribute $2^5 = 32$. Thus, $X$ must be a multiple of 32 (e.g., 32 or 160).

From Statement I:

$X$ is even. This does not uniquely fix $X$ ($X$ could be 32 or 160). Not sufficient.

From Statement II:

$X$ is a multiple of 5. Since $X$ must also contain $2^5 = 32$, $X$ must be a multiple of $32 \times 5 = 160$.

Since $\text{LCM}(16, X, 40) = 160$, the maximum value $X$ can take is 160. Hence $X = 160$ uniquely.

Statement II alone is sufficient.

✓ Correct Option: (b) The data in statement II alone is sufficient to answer the question, while the data in statement I alone is not sufficient to answer the question.

Q44 (Set 2)

In the following question, two quantities are given. Compare the quantities and choose the correct option.

$x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$

$2y^2 - 9y + 9 = 0$

Quantity I: Product of the largest roots of both equations.

Quantity II: $\sqrt[3]{729}$

  1. Quantity I > Quantity II
  2. Quantity I < Quantity II
  3. Quantity I $\ge$ Quantity II
  4. Quantity I $\le$ Quantity II
  5. Quantity I = Quantity II or relation cannot be established
▶ Solution

Equation 1:

$$x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$$ $$(x - 2)(x - 3) = 0$$ $$x = 2, 3$$

Largest root of equation 1 is $3$.

Equation 2:

$$2y^2 - 9y + 9 = 0$$ $$2y^2 - 6y - 3y + 9 = 0$$ $$2y(y - 3) - 3(y - 3) = 0$$ $$(2y - 3)(y - 3) = 0$$ $$y = 1.5, 3$$

Largest root of equation 2 is $3$.

Quantity I:

$$\text{Product of largest roots} = 3 \times 3 = 9$$

Quantity II:

$$\sqrt[3]{729} = 9$$

Therefore, Quantity I = Quantity II.

✓ Correct Option: (e) Quantity I = Quantity II or relation cannot be established

Profit $\propto$ Investment $\times$ Time. Always compute investment-time products before forming ratios.

Q64 (Set 1)

A and B started a business with investments in the ratio of 3 : 4 respectively. Out of the total annual profit of Rs. 5100, A’s share was Rs. 2700. If the difference between the periods for which A and B invested is 3 months, for how many months did A invest?

  1. 6
  2. 8
  3. 9
  4. 12
  5. 10
▶ Solution

Total profit $=$ Rs. 5100

A's share $=$ Rs. 2700

B's share $= 5100 - 2700 =$ Rs. 2400

Ratio of profit of A to B $= 2700 : 2400 = 9 : 8$

Let investment of A $= 3x$ and B $= 4x$.

Let investment time of A $= T_A$ months and B $= T_B$ months.

Profit ratio:

$$\frac{3x \times T_A}{4x \times T_B} = \frac{9}{8}$$ $$\frac{T_A}{T_B} = \frac{9}{8} \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{3}{2}$$

Given difference between investment periods is 3 months:

$$T_A - T_B = 3$$

Since $\frac{T_A}{T_B} = \frac{3}{2}$, let $T_A = 3k$ and $T_B = 2k$.

$$3k - 2k = 3 \Rightarrow k = 3$$ $$T_A = 3 \times 3 = 9\text{ months}$$

✓ Correct Option: (c) 9

Q41 (Set 2)

A and B started a business with capitals in the ratio 7 : 9. After 6 months, C joined them with capital twice that of A. At the end of the year, total profit was Rs. 92000. Find the combined share of profit of B and C.

  1. Rs. 56000
  2. Rs. 64000
  3. Rs. 72000
  4. Rs. 48000
  5. Rs. 60000
▶ Solution

Let capitals of A and B be $7x$ and $9x$ respectively.

Capital of C $= 2 \times 7x = 14x$.

Time period of investment:

A invested for 12 months.

B invested for 12 months.

C invested for 6 months.

Ratio of profit shares:

$$\text{A} : \text{B} : \text{C} = (7x \times 12) : (9x \times 12) : (14x \times 6)$$ $$\text{A} : \text{B} : \text{C} = 84x : 108x : 84x$$ $$\text{A} : \text{B} : \text{C} = 7 : 9 : 7$$

Total ratio parts $= 7 + 9 + 7 = 23$.

Combined share of B and C $= 9 + 7 = 16$ parts.

$$\text{Combined share} = \frac{16}{23} \times 92000 = 16 \times 4000 = \text{Rs. } 64000$$

✓ Correct Option: (b) Rs. 64000

Q65 (Set 3)

A started a business by investing Rs. 2400 and B joined him by investing Rs. X. After 9 months, A added Rs. 600 while B withdrew Rs. 600. If at the end of the year, the ratio of profit of A to B is 17 : 23, then find the value of X.

  1. 2400
  2. 3000
  3. 2800
  4. 3200
  5. 3600
▶ Solution

Total equivalent investment of A for 1 year:

$$= 2400 \times 9 + (2400 + 600) \times 3$$ $$= 21600 + 3000 \times 3$$ $$= 21600 + 9000 = 30600$$

Total equivalent investment of B for 1 year:

$$= X \times 9 + (X - 600) \times 3$$ $$= 9X + 3X - 1800$$ $$= 12X - 1800$$

Given profit ratio of A to B is 17 : 23:

$$\frac{30600}{12X - 1800} = \frac{17}{23}$$ $$\frac{1800}{12X - 1800} = \frac{1}{23} \quad (\text{dividing numerator by } 17)$$ $$1800 \times 23 = 12X - 1800$$ $$41400 = 12X - 1800$$ $$12X = 43200$$ $$X = 3600$$

✓ Correct Option: (e) 3600

Q63 (Set 7)

Question 58 (Set 1)

A man spends 12% of his monthly salary on education and 7% on house rent. Out of the remaining salary, he invests Rs. 10250 in SIP and saves $4x\%$ of his total monthly salary. If his monthly salary is Rs. 25000, find the value of $x$.

  5. 6

Monthly salary $=$ Rs. 25000

Amount spent on education and rent:

$$= 19\% \text{ of } 25000 = \frac{19}{100} \times 25000 = \text{Rs. } 4750$$

Remaining salary after education and rent:

$$= 25000 - 4750 = \text{Rs. } 20250$$

Amount invested in SIP $=$ Rs. 10250

Remaining savings:

$$= 20250 - 10250 = \text{Rs. } 10000$$

Savings as percentage of total salary:

$$\text{Savings \%} = \frac{10000}{25000} \times 100 = 40\%$$

Given savings percentage is $4x\%$:

$$4x = 40 \Rightarrow x = 10$$ Tip: Convert all known expenses to percentages first; the remainder is the unknown.

✓ Correct Option: (d) 10

Question 70 (Set 2)

Amount spent on travel $= \frac{1}{2} \times 0.8R = 0.4R$.

$$\% \text{ more} = \frac{18000 - 5000}{5000} \times 100 = \frac{13000}{5000} \times 100 = 260\%$$ Tip: When half is saved and half is spent on travel, they are equal.

✓ Correct Option: (b) 260%
