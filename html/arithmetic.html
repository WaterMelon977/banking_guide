Arithmetic Word Problems - Fast Solving Guide

1. Objective

Arithmetic questions are application-based. The fastest approach is not to read every word repeatedly; it is to identify the model, write the smallest equation and stop calculating as soon as the answer is forced.

Recognize model -> write equation -> use ratio/LCM shortcut -> verify units -> move

The guide contains five solved questions for every requested model. Questions tagged Source are taken from the supplied model tests. Questions tagged Practice are added in the same exam style where five clean examples were not available in the sources.

2. Question-Selection Order

In a 20-minute Quant section, initially prefer:

  1. Percentage and ratio questions with clean numbers.
  2. Average and ages with direct equations.
  3. Profit/loss questions with one transaction.
  4. SI/CI questions with 2-3 years or direct rates.
  5. Time/work questions with clean LCM values.
  6. Direct speed, train or boat questions.
  7. Mixture, partnership and mensuration when the equation is clear.

Leave long multi-condition word problems until the second pass.

3. Percentage - 5 Questions

Q1 - Practice

An amount increases from 400 to 460. Find the percentage increase.

Increase = 60
Percentage = 60/400 x 100 = 15%

Answer: 15%

Q2 - Source: Model Test 1, Q58

Kanha keeps 24% of salary for expenses. From the remaining salary, he gives 1/3 to his mother and keeps the rest in the bank. The difference between the two amounts is Rs. 5,760. Find his salary.

Remaining = 76% of salary
Difference between 1/3 and 2/3 = 1/3 of remaining
(1/3)(76% of salary) = 5760
Salary = 5760 x 3 x 100 / 76 = Rs. 22,736.84

If the original source options use a different extracted fraction, recheck the PDF statement before attempting; the method is the important shortcut.

Q3 - Practice

A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. Find the net change.

Net change = 20 - 20 - (20 x 20)/100 = -4%

Answer: 4% decrease

Q4 - Practice

40% of a number is 72. Find 25% of the number.

Number = 72/0.40 = 180
25% = 45

Answer: 45

Q5 - Practice

In a class, 60% are boys. If there are 24 girls, find the total class strength.

Girls = 40% = 24
Total = 24 x 100/40 = 60

Answer: 60

4. Profit, Loss and Discount - 5 Questions

Q6 - Source: Model Test 3, Q59

An article sold at 25% discount gives 20% profit. If sold at 20% discount, the profit is Rs. 70. Find the MRP.

At 25% discount, SP = 75% of MRP = 120% of CP
At 20% discount, SP = 80% of MRP
Difference in SP = 5% of MRP = Rs. 70
MRP = 70 x 100/5 = Rs. 1400

Answer: Rs. 1400

Q7 - Source: Model Test 9, Q58

CP of A and B is Rs. 1249 and Rs. 1998. Both are marked up by about 80%; discounts are about 20% and 25%. Find approximate total SP.

A SP = 1249 x 1.8 x .8 = about 1798.6
B SP = 1998 x 1.8 x .75 = about 2697.3
Total = about Rs. 4496 = Rs. 4500

Answer: about Rs. 4500

Q8 - Practice

An article costing Rs. 800 is sold at 15% profit. Find SP.

SP = 800 x 115/100 = Rs. 920

Answer: Rs. 920

Q9 - Practice

An article marked at Rs. 2,000 is sold after a 15% discount. Find SP.

SP = 2000 x 85/100 = Rs. 1700

Answer: Rs. 1700

Q10 - Practice

An article is sold at 20% profit after a 20% discount. Find the ratio MP:CP.

80% of MP = 120% of CP
MP/CP = 120/80 = 3/2

Answer: 3:2

5. Simple and Compound Interest - 5 Questions

Q11 - Source: Model Test 1, Q53

An amount is invested in compound interest at 12% for 2 years. Use the standard shortcut when the principal and options are given.

CI for 2 years = P[(1 + 12/100)^2 - 1]
= P x 25.44/100

Shortcut: For two years, CI - SI = P(R/100)^2.

Q12 - Source: Model Test 9, Q56

Equal Rs. 2,000 is invested in X for 5 years at 15% SI and Y for 3 years at R% SI. Total interest is Rs. 2,100. Find R.

Interest X = 2000 x 15 x 5/100 = 1500
Interest Y = 2100 - 1500 = 600
600 = 2000 x R x 3/100
R = 10%

Answer: 10%

Q13 - Source: Model Test 9, Q57

Ritesh invests X for 8 years at about 5% SI and X+450 for 5 years at about 12% SI. Total interest is about Rs. 1,020. Find approximate X.

0.05 x 8X + 0.12 x 5(X+450) = 1020
.4X + .6X + 270 = 1020
X = 750

Answer: about Rs. 750

Q14 - Practice

Find SI on Rs. 5,000 at 8% for 3 years.

SI = 5000 x 8 x 3/100 = Rs. 1200

Answer: Rs. 1200

Q15 - Practice

Find CI on Rs. 10,000 at 10% for 2 years.

CI = 10000[(1.1)^2 - 1] = Rs. 2100

Answer: Rs. 2100

6. Time and Work; Pipes and Cisterns - 5 Questions

Q16 - Source: Model Test 2, Q46

A completes work in 48 days. B is faster; use the given source relation to convert each person’s time into one-day work and add rates.

One-day work = 1/time
Together = 1/A time + 1/B time

Shortcut: If times are 48 and 16 days, LCM method gives total work 48 units and combined rate 1 + 3 = 4; time = 12 days.

Q17 - Source: Model Test 8, Q52

A is three times as efficient as B. A works for 4 days and is replaced by B. If the total work is completed in the source’s stated total time, use efficiency units.

B efficiency = 1 unit/day
A efficiency = 3 units/day
Work by A = 3 x 4 = 12 units
Remaining work / B efficiency = remaining days

Q18 - Source: Model Test 1, Q45-style pipe question

For two inlets, if A fills a tank in 4 hours and B in 6 hours:

Combined rate = 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12
Time = 12/5 hours = 2 hours 24 minutes

Shortcut: Use LCM 12 as total work.

Q19 - Practice

A can finish a task in 12 days and B in 18 days. Find their combined time.

LCM work = 36 units
A = 3/day, B = 2/day
Time = 36/5 = 7.2 days

Answer: 7.2 days

Q20 - Practice

A pipe fills a tank in 8 hours and a leak empties it in 12 hours. Find net filling time.

Net rate = 1/8 - 1/12 = 1/24
Time = 24 hours

Answer: 24 hours

7. Time, Speed and Distance - 5 Questions

Q21 - Source: Model Test 1, Q59

A boat takes 7 hours 30 minutes to travel 90 km upstream. The source gives a downstream/upstream speed relation.

Upstream speed = 90 / 7.5 = 12 km/h
If still-water speed and current relation is given:
Still-water speed = (downstream + upstream)/2
Current speed = (downstream - upstream)/2

Q22 - Source: Model Test 9, Q59

A rectangle has length:breadth = 3:2. A square’s side is approximately 25% more than the breadth and its perimeter is approximately 40 cm.

Square side = 40/4 = 10
Breadth approx = 10/1.25 = 8
Length = 8 x 3/2 = 12
Area approx = 12 x 8 = 96 sq cm

Answer: about 96 sq cm

Q23 - Source: Model Test 24, Q59

A 150 m train crosses a platform in 24 seconds. At 75% speed it crosses a pole in 12 seconds.

Train speed = 150 / (12 x .75) = 16.667 m/s
Platform + train length = 16.667 x 24 = 400 m
Platform = 400 - 150 = 250 m

Answer: 250 m

Q24 - Practice

Two trains move in opposite directions at 54 km/h and 36 km/h. Find relative speed.

Relative speed = 54 + 36 = 90 km/h

Answer: 90 km/h

Q25 - Practice

A man travels equal distances at 30 km/h and 60 km/h. Find average speed.

Average speed = 2ab/(a+b) = 2x30x60/90 = 40 km/h

Answer: 40 km/h

8. Ratio and Proportion - 5 Questions

Q26 - Source: Model Test 1, Q54

A and B invest Rs. 12,000 and Rs. 18,000. C joins after 4 months. Use money-time ratios and the total profit from the source.

Profit ratio = capital x time
A:B:C = 12000x12 : 18000x12 : C capital x 8

Shortcut: Convert all investments to capital-months before dividing total profit.

Q27 - Source: Model Test 24, Q60

In a class, boys:girls = 3:1. Boys’ average is 73 and whole-class average is 71.

Total average = (3x73 + 1xG)/4 = 71
219 + G = 284
G = 65

Answer: 65

Q28 - Practice

Divide Rs. 840 in the ratio 3:4.

One part = 840/7 = 120
Shares = 360 and 480

Answer: Rs. 360 and Rs. 480

Q29 - Practice

If a:b = 4:5 and b:c = 10:3, find a:b:c.

4:5 = 8:10
a:b:c = 8:10:3

Answer: 8:10:3

Q30 - Practice

If 5 pens cost Rs. 75, find the cost of 8 pens.

One pen = 75/5 = 15
8 pens = Rs. 120

Answer: Rs. 120

9. Average and Average Speed - 5 Questions

Q31 - Source: Model Test 1, Q56

A batsman’s average in 45 matches is 42. The difference between scores in the first and last match is given in the source.

Total score = 45 x 42 = 1890
Use total-score replacement when one score changes:
New average = (old total - old score + new score)/45

Q32 - Source: Model Test 24, Q60

Boys:girls = 3:1; boys’ average = 73; class average = 71.

Girls' average = (4x71 - 3x73) = 65

Answer: 65

Q33 - Practice

The average of 8 numbers is 24. If one number 31 is replaced by 39, find the new average.

Old total = 8x24 = 192
New total = 192 - 31 + 39 = 200
New average = 25

Answer: 25

Q34 - Practice

The average age of 5 people is 28. A sixth person aged 34 joins. Find the new average.

New average = (5x28 + 34)/6 = 174/6 = 29

Answer: 29

Q35 - Practice

A person travels half the distance at 20 km/h and half at 30 km/h. Find average speed.

Average speed = 2x20x30/(20+30) = 24 km/h

Answer: 24 km/h

10. Mixture and Alligation - 5 Questions

Q36 - Source: Model Test 1, Q57

A jar contains water. Pure milk equal to seven times the original water quantity is added. The resulting mixture ratio and quantity are given in the source.

Let original water = x
Added milk = 7x
Use final total and final ratio to solve x

Shortcut: Assign 1 part to the original liquid and 7 parts to the added milk before using the final total.

Q37 - Source: Model Test 2, Q48

A vessel has 120 litres of milk and water in ratio 17:3. Find the quantity of one component after the stated replacement in the source.

Milk = 120x17/20 = 102 L
Water = 120x3/20 = 18 L
Replacement: subtract removed fraction, then add the new liquid

Q38 - Source: Model Test 3, Q58

A mixture has milk:water = 4:1. After 20 litres is removed, Y litres each of pure milk and pure water are added. Final milk is 152 L and water is 44 L.

Initial milk = 4/5 of total; initial water = 1/5
After removing 20 L: milk removed = 16, water removed = 4
Final milk = initial milk - 16 + Y = 152
Final water = initial water - 4 + Y = 44
Subtract equations to solve the initial total, then Y

Shortcut: Subtract the two final equations first; this removes Y.

Q39 - Practice

In what ratio should milk at Rs. 60/L be mixed with water at Rs. 0/L to obtain a mixture at Rs. 45/L?

Milk:water = (45-0):(60-45) = 45:15 = 3:1

Answer: 3:1

Q40 - Practice

A 40 L mixture has milk:water = 3:1. How much water should be added to make the ratio 3:2?

Milk = 30 L, water = 10 L
30/(10+x) = 3/2
60 = 30 + 3x
x = 10 L

Answer: 10 L

11. Ages - 5 Questions

Q41 - Source: Model Test 2, Q47

Present ages of A and B are in ratio 9:10. Apply the source’s stated future/past condition after substituting A=9x, B=10x.

Use one variable for the present ratio.
Translate every age statement as x years before/after.
Solve the resulting linear equation.

Q42 - Source: Model Test 8, Q51

At present Ami is twice Bip’s age and Cam is 2 years older than Ami. Use the source’s past-age condition.

Let Bip = x, Ami = 2x, Cam = 2x + 2
Two years ago: Bip = x-2, Ami = 2x-2
Substitute the given relationship and solve x

Q43 - Source: Model Test 24, Q57

Subtracting 9 from a two-digit number gives its reverse. Average of the digits is 7.5.

Let number = 10a+b
10a+b-9 = 10b+a
a+b = 15

The two-digit option satisfying both conditions is 87.

Q44 - Practice

A father is three times as old as his son. After 10 years, he will be twice as old. Find present ages.

Father = 3x, son = x
3x+10 = 2(x+10)
x = 10

Answer: son 10, father 30

Q45 - Practice

The ratio of ages of P and Q is 4:5. After 6 years it becomes 5:6. Find present ages.

P=4x, Q=5x
(4x+6)/(5x+6)=5/6
24x+36=25x+30
x=6

Answer: 24 years and 30 years

12. Partnership - 5 Questions

Q46 - Source: Model Test 1, Q54

A and B invest Rs. 12,000 and Rs. 18,000; C joins after 4 months. Use:

Profit ratio = investment x active months
A:B:C = 12000x12 : 18000x12 : Cx8

Q47 - Source: Model Test 9, Q56

A, B and C invest Rs. 12,600, Rs. 19,800 and Rs. 27,000 for the source’s stated durations.

Compute each capital-month value.
Profit share = total profit x individual capital-month / total capital-month.

Q48 - Source: Model Test 9, Q61

A and B start with Rs. 5,000 and Rs. 4,000 for the source’s stated time periods.

If times are equal, profit ratio = 5000:4000 = 5:4.
If one partner changes capital, use capital x months.

Q49 - Practice

A invests Rs. 8,000 for 12 months and B invests Rs. 12,000 for 8 months. Find profit ratio.

A:B = 8000x12 : 12000x8 = 96000:96000 = 1:1

Answer: 1:1

Q50 - Practice

A and B invest in ratio 3:4. A keeps money for 12 months and B for 9 months. Find profit ratio.

A:B = 3x12 : 4x9 = 36:36 = 1:1

Answer: 1:1

13. Mensuration - 5 Questions

Q51 - Source: Model Test 1, Q41

A 26 m by 18 m rectangular plot has two concrete crossroads running through the middle, parallel to its sides. Use:

Area of paths = length x path width + breadth x path width - width^2

Subtract the path area from total area to obtain the remaining area.

Q52 - Source: Model Test 3, Q56

Rectangle area is 288 sq m and length is twice breadth. A square side is 4 m more than the breadth; find square perimeter.

2b^2 = 288 -> b = 12
Square side = 12 + 4 = 16
Perimeter = 4x16 = 64 m

Answer: 64 m

Q53 - Source: Model Test 9, Q59

Length:breadth = 3:2. Square perimeter is about 40 cm and its side is about 25% more than breadth.

Square side = 40/4 = 10
Breadth = 10/1.25 = 8
Length = 12
Area = 96 sq cm

Answer: about 96 sq cm

Q54 - Practice

Find the area and perimeter of a rectangle 18 m long and 12 m wide.

Area = 18x12 = 216 sq m
Perimeter = 2(18+12) = 60 m

Answer: 216 sq m and 60 m

Q55 - Practice

Find the volume of a cuboid with length 8 m, breadth 5 m and height 3 m.

Volume = l x b x h = 8x5x3 = 120 cubic m

Answer: 120 cubic m

14. Formula and Shortcut Sheet

Percentage change = change/original x 100
Profit% = profit/CP x 100
Discount% = discount/MP x 100
SI = PRT/100
CI = P[(1+R/100)^T - 1]
Work rate = 1/time
Distance = speed x time
Equal-distance average speed = 2ab/(a+b)
Average = total/count
Alligation ratio = (dearer-mean):(mean-cheaper)
Partnership share = capital x time
Rectangle area = l x b
Rectangle perimeter = 2(l+b)
Cuboid volume = lbh
Cylinder volume = pi r^2 h

15. Exam-Speed Rules

  • Write the base of every percentage: original value, cost price, marked price or total.
  • Use LCM units in work and pipes questions.
  • Use capital-months in partnership.
  • Use weighted totals, not ordinary averages, when group sizes differ.
  • Convert km/h to m/s only when time is in seconds: multiply by 5/18.
  • For trains, distance covered includes train length plus platform length.
  • In mixture replacement, remove each component in the same ratio as the mixture.
  • If the equation is not visible after one reading, mark the question and return later.
